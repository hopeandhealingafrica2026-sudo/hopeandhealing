Ubufatanye no Gushyira Hamwe

Guhuza abantu, ubumenyi n'ibikoresho mu iterambere rirambye.

HHA-RU yizera ko impinduka nyayo igerwaho binyuze mu bufatanye. Dufatanya n'imiryango, amashuri, amatorero, za Leta n'abantu ku giti cyabo kugira ngo duhe icyizere n'ubuvuzi bw'imitima abaturage ba Kabarore na Nakivale.

Dufatanya na nde?

  • Imiryango itegamiye kuri Leta (NGOs) n'imiryango mpuzamahanga
  • Amashuri, amatorero n'amashyirahamwe y'abaturage
  • Za Leta, ibigo by'ubuzima n'ibigo by'uburezi
  • Abaterankunga, abacuruzi n'abantu ku giti cyabo

Ubufatanye bukora iki?

Ubufatanye bwacu bugamije gufasha urubyiruko kwiga ikoranabuhanga (HDMT), gufasha abagore kwihangira imirimo, no gufasha abaturage gukira ibikomere by'imitima. Buri mufatanyabikorwa agira uruhare rwe mu guhindura ubuzima bw'abantu.

Kugeza ubu abafatanyabikorwa bacu ni:

1. HDMT — Hope Digital Mobility Tech

Ubufatanye mu kwigisha urubyiruko ikoranabuhanga, digital skills, no kubona amahirwe y'akazi.

Sura HDMT ↗

2. CoderBC

Ubufatanye mu kwiga coding no kubaka websites — guha urubyiruko ubumenyi bw'ikoranabuhanga.

Sura CoderBC ↗

3. Nzozi Community

Ubufatanye mu kubaka community, gufasha urubyiruko, gusangira icyizere no gukorera hamwe iterambere.

Sura Nzozi Community ↗

4. Nzozi Pure Blend

Ubufatanye mu mibereho n'ubucuruzi — gufasha abagore n'urubyiruko kwihangira imirimo binyuze mu bicuruzwa by'umwimerere.

Sura Nzozi Pure Blend ↗

Niba namwe mushaka kwifatanya natwe

Niba namwe mushaka kwifatanya natwe gufatanya mu bikorwa byo kuruhura imitima no kugarura icyizere mu bantu, tubafasha kwigira — mwatugana.

Uburyo bwo gufatanya natwe

  1. Gutera inkunga imishinga yacu — HDMT, Urubyiruko, Abagore
  2. Kuduha ibikoresho — mudasobwa, ibitabo, ibikoresho by'ishuri
  3. Kuba umujyanama cyangwa umukorerabushake
  4. Gusura imishinga yacu i Kabarore na Nakivale
