Partnerships & Collaboration

Connecting People, Expertise and Resources for Sustainable Development

HHA-RU believes lasting development is achieved when communities, institutions and organisations work together. We seek partnerships that strengthen local capacities, expand opportunities and support practical solutions for refugees, host communities, women and young people.

We welcome NGOs, government institutions, development agencies, foundations, donors, universities, research institutions, vocational centres, private-sector companies, technology organisations and social enterprises.

HHA-RU brings community-based experience, local relationships, knowledge of refugee and host community needs, coordination and access to practical initiatives in Rwanda and Uganda.

Areas: vocational and digital skills, automotive technology, youth entrepreneurship, women's empowerment, livelihoods, food and beverage enterprise, quality improvement, market access and capacity strengthening.

Each partnership will be explored according to shared objectives, community needs, available resources and agreed responsibilities.

Guhuza abantu, ubumenyi n'ibikoresho mu iterambere rirambye

HHA-RU yemera ko iterambere rirambye rigerwaho igihe abaturage, inzego n'imiryango bakoranye.

Twishimira gukorana n'imiryango itegamiye kuri Leta, inzego za Leta, ibigo biteza imbere imishinga, fondasiyo, abaterankunga, kaminuza, ibigo by'ubushakashatsi, amashuri y'imyuga, ibigo by'ubucuruzi, ibigo by'ikoranabuhanga n'imishinga y'abaturage.

HHA-RU izana ubunararibonye mu baturage, imikoranire, ubumenyi ku bibazo by'impunzi n'abaturage baturanye na zo, guhuza ibikorwa n'amahirwe mu Rwanda no muri Uganda.

Inzego: imyuga n'ikoranabuhanga, automotive, kwihangira imirimo kw'urubyiruko, guteza imbere abagore, imibereho, ibiribwa n'ibinyobwa, ubuziranenge, amasoko no kongerera ubushobozi inzego.
