Women and Community Empowerment

Women and Community Empowerment is a core programme of HHA-RU in Uganda. Building on our experience supporting women in Rwanda, we promote women's economic empowerment, practical skills development, income-generating opportunities and stronger communities.

Our work in Nakivale and Isingiro helps women develop practical abilities that can improve household livelihoods and create opportunities for greater economic independence.

We encourage collective action through group activities, small-business support and community savings approaches. Our aim is sustainable income opportunities rather than one-time assistance.

We also recognise that economic empowerment is connected to emotional well-being and social support.

HHA-RU welcomes partnerships that can strengthen women's skills, improve product quality, expand market access and support women-led income-generating groups.

Autonomisation des Femmes et des Communautés

L'Autonomisation des Femmes et des Communautés est un programme central de HHA-RU en Ouganda. Nous promouvons l'autonomisation économique des femmes, les compétences pratiques, les revenus et des communautés plus fortes.

Notre travail à Nakivale et à Isingiro aide les femmes à développer des capacités pratiques pour améliorer les moyens de subsistance et l'indépendance économique.

Nous encourageons l'action collective, le soutien aux petites entreprises et l'épargne communautaire.

HHA-RU accueille les partenariats qui renforcent les compétences, la qualité, l'accès aux marchés et les groupes dirigés par des femmes.

Guteza Imbere Abagore n'Abaturage

Guteza imbere abagore n'abaturage ni imwe muri gahunda z'ingenzi za HHA-RU muri Uganda. Dushingiye ku bunararibonye twagize mu gufasha abagore mu Rwanda, dukomeza guteza imbere ubushobozi bw'abagore mu bukungu, ubumenyi ngiro, ibikorwa bibyara inyungu no kubaka abaturage bafite imibereho myiza.

Ibikorwa byacu muri Nakivale no mu Karere ka Isingiro bigamije gufasha abagore kunguka ubumenyi bushobora guteza imbere imibereho y'imiryango yabo no kubongerera ubushobozi bwo kwigira mu bukungu.

Dushishikariza abagore gukorera hamwe, gushyigikira ubucuruzi buciriritse no guteza imbere uburyo bwo kuzigama.

HHA-RU yifuza gukorana n'abafatanyabikorwa mu guteza imbere ubumenyi, kunoza ubwiza bw'ibicuruzwa, kubafungurira amasoko no gushyigikira amatsinda y'abagore.
